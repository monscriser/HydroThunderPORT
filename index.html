DEFY THE WATER.
CHASE THE BOOST.

Hydro Thunder Hurricane is known for fantastical race locations, massive jumps, secret shortcuts and transforming rocket-powered speedboats. The original game features modes such as Race, Ring Master, Gauntlet and Championship.

This site documents HydroThunderPORT, an unofficial PC port project—not a redistribution of the original game. Features in the Xbox release are not necessarily all functional in this experimental port.
